1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Barima Automobile & Transportation Services ("Barima", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information. It covers this website, our vehicle sourcing service and our B-Drive transportation service.

We are based in Ontario, Canada, and we follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and its fair information principles. Where we serve clients in Ghana, we also respect Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Where we serve clients in Quebec, we also respect Quebec's privacy law.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need, and mostly what you choose to give us when you call, WhatsApp or email us, or use a form on this website. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Details of the vehicle you want, such as make, model, year and budget, and the country you need it in
  • For B-Drive, pick-up and drop-off addresses, date and time, flight details for airport transfers, the number of passengers and any accessibility or child seat needs
  • Identification needed to buy, register or export a vehicle in your name, such as a driver's licence, passport or Ghana Card
  • Payment records, and delivery or shipping details for your vehicle
  • Messages and call notes about your request or booking

If you give us information about someone else, such as a passenger or the person receiving a vehicle, please make sure they agree to it.

4. How the website forms work

The forms on this website do not store your information on our website. When you press send, the form opens your own email app, with your details filled in for you to review and send. We receive your information only if you choose to send it. Your email or WhatsApp message is then held by those services, as explained below.

5. How we use your information and your consent

  • To respond to your enquiries and give you quotes
  • To find, buy, export and deliver or ship the vehicle you request
  • To arrange, confirm and carry out B-Drive rides
  • To meet our legal, accounting, tax, insurance and export obligations
  • To contact you about your request, purchase or booking

We use your information only for the purposes we tell you about, or for purposes you would reasonably expect. By contacting us and asking for a service, you consent to us using your information to provide it. We will ask for your express consent for anything else, and before we collect sensitive information such as identification documents.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. If you do, we may no longer be able to provide the service.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to provide our services. This can include:

  • Auction houses, dealers and sellers, to buy the vehicle
  • Shipping lines, freight forwarders, customs brokers and agents, to export and ship vehicles to Ghana
  • The Canada Border Services Agency and other authorities, to export a vehicle or where the law requires it
  • Payment providers, insurers, our accountant and our professional advisers
  • Our email and messaging providers, Google (Gmail) and Meta (WhatsApp), which hold our messages with you

We require the parties we work with to protect your information and use it only for the job we give them. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Storing and sending information outside Canada

Some of your information is stored or handled outside Canada. Our email (Gmail, provided by Google) and WhatsApp (provided by Meta) may store messages on servers in the United States and other countries. Vehicle purchase and shipping details are shared with shipping agents, clearing agents and authorities in Ghana for clients there.

When your information is outside Canada, it is subject to the laws of that country, and courts, law enforcement and national security authorities there may be able to access it. You can contact our privacy officer to ask about our practices for service providers outside Canada.

8. Marketing messages

We send you messages about your enquiry, purchase or booking. We send promotional messages, such as offers or newsletters, only if you have agreed to receive them, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Every promotional message will say who it is from and give you an easy way to unsubscribe, and we will act on your request within 10 business days.

9. Cookies and third parties

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not use analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address. Links to WhatsApp open WhatsApp, which handles your information under its own privacy policy.

10. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires:

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a purchase or ride: up to 24 months
  • Purchase, payment, export and tax records: at least 6 years, as Canadian tax law requires
  • Copies of identification documents: only as long as needed for the purchase, registration or export, then deleted

If we use your information to make a decision about you, we keep it long enough for you to ask to see it. After that, we delete or destroy it securely.

11. How we protect it

We protect your information with safeguards suited to how sensitive it is. Only people who need your information to provide your service can see it. We protect our email and messaging accounts with strong passwords and two-step verification, and we keep paper records in a secure place. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, however, so please do not send full card numbers by email or WhatsApp.

12. If there is a data breach

If a breach of security involving your information creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you as soon as possible, report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and tell you what you can do to protect yourself. We keep a record of every breach, as PIPEDA requires.

13. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with
  • Get a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Withdraw your consent, subject to legal and contractual limits

Send your request to our privacy officer. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond within 30 days, and we do not charge for this. If we can't give you some information, we will explain why.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Clients in Quebec can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, and clients in Ghana can also contact Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

14. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18, except details a parent or guardian gives us to arrange a ride for their child.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell clients we are working with.
