**B-Drive.** We provide private transportation in Ontario, Canada. This includes pick-up and drop-off, airport transfers, rides to hospitals, emergency units and railway stations, corporate and personal transportation, and parcel deliveries.

## 5. Your purchase agreement

A purchase goes ahead only once you have agreed to it in writing. Before you pay anything, we will give you a written agreement that sets out:

- Our business name, address, phone number and email

- What happens to your deposit, and your cancellation and refund rights

We will give you a copy of the signed agreement, and you can ask us for another copy at any time.

## 7. Deposits, cancellations and refunds

- **If we can't buy the vehicle.** If we don't win the auction or can't find a suitable vehicle within the agreed time, you can choose to have your deposit fully refunded or used for another vehicle. Refunds are paid within one week to the account you paid from.
- **If you cancel before we buy.** You can cancel before we have bid on or bought a vehicle for you. We will refund your deposit within one week, minus any costs set out in your agreement that we have already paid on your behalf.
- **After we buy.** Once a vehicle has been bought for you, the purchase can't be cancelled, because auction and private sales are final. Auction and buyer fees already paid can't be refunded.

These terms do not reduce any cancellation or refund rights you have under Ontario consumer protection law.

## 8. Vehicle condition and disclosure

Before you commit to a vehicle, we will tell you in writing everything we know, or should reasonably know, that matters to your decision. This includes:

- Whether the vehicle has a branded title, such as salvage, rebuilt or irreparable
- Accident or damage history, including the amount of any insurance claim we know of
- Previous use as a taxi, rental, police or emergency vehicle
- Whether the odometer reading may be wrong
- Whether the vehicle was previously registered outside Ontario or Canada

Vehicles from insurance auctions often have damage or a branded title. We cannot guarantee the condition of a vehicle beyond what we have disclosed and what the seller or auction discloses. If a vehicle is sold "as is", we will say so in your agreement in the words required by law, and ask you to sign to confirm you understand. Please ask questions and, where possible, arrange an independent inspection before you agree to buy.

## 9. Shipping to Ghana, duty and clearing

For clients in Ghana, we arrange export from Canada and shipping to Ghana. We report the vehicle to the Canada Border Services Agency before export as Canadian law requires. Shipping times are estimates only and can be affected by shipping lines, ports, weather and customs. Marine cargo insurance is available on request and is recommended.

Ghana has its own rules on which vehicles can be imported, including rules on vehicle age and on salvaged or accident-damaged vehicles. Please check that your vehicle can be imported before you buy, and we will help where we can.

When the vehicle arrives in Ghana, the client is responsible for clearing it. This includes paying import duty, taxes, port charges and any other clearing costs, and meeting the requirements of the Ghana Revenue Authority and other authorities. Barima is not responsible for duty, clearing costs or delays at the port.

For clients in Canada, we arrange the vehicle for you here. Registration, insurance, safety certification, emissions or federal import inspections where they apply, and any taxes due in your province are your responsibility unless we agree otherwise in writing.

## 11. B-Drive bookings and fares

B-Drive rides are booked by phone call or WhatsApp only, on +1 (416) 605-6280. A booking is confirmed only when we confirm it to you, and we will tell you the fare before we confirm.

- **Payment.** Fares can be paid by credit or debit card, Interac e-Transfer, wire transfer or cash. HST is included in the fare we quote unless we say otherwise.
- **Waiting time.** We include 10 minutes of free waiting time, and 45 minutes for airport pick-ups after your flight lands. After that, waiting time may be charged at a per-minute rate, which we will tell you when you book.
- **Cancellations.** You can cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before your pick-up time. Later cancellations may be charged up to 50% of the fare, and a no-show may be charged the full fare.
- **Our cancellations.** If we have to cancel, we will tell you as soon as possible and refund anything you've paid for that ride.

Please give accurate pick-up and drop-off details and times. Travel times are estimates and can be affected by traffic, weather and road conditions.

B-Drive is a private transportation service, not an ambulance or emergency medical service. In a medical emergency, call 911.

## 12. Children and car seats

Please tell us when booking if a child is travelling. Children must ride in a car seat or booster seat suited to their age, weight and height. Unless we agree to provide one, the parent or guardian must bring and fit it. A child under 16 must travel with an adult unless we agree otherwise in advance.

If a passenger causes damage or makes the vehicle need special cleaning, we may charge the reasonable cost, up to $150 for cleaning, plus the reasonable cost of repairing any damage, and will show you the cost first.
