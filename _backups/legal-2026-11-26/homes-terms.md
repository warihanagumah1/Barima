## 6. Buyers who are not Ghanaian citizens

Under Ghana's Constitution and the Land Act, 2020, a person who is not a Ghanaian citizen cannot own land outright (freehold). Non-citizens can hold land on a lease of up to 50 years, which may be renewed. If you are not a Ghanaian citizen, including if you hold only Canadian or another foreign citizenship, your sale agreement will grant a lease on these terms. Ghanaians with dual citizenship keep their rights as Ghanaian citizens. Please tell us your citizenship before you commit, and take legal advice if you are unsure.
