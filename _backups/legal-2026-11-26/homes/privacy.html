We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). 3. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need, mostly what you choose to give us when you call or WhatsApp us, or use the request form on this website. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Whether you want land or a house, your preferred area, your budget and any other details you share
  • Identification needed to prepare a sale and register the property, such as a Ghana Card or passport, and proof of citizenship where it affects the type of title
  • Details of any representative or lawyer you appoint, and the power of attorney or authority you give them
  • Payment records and copies of receipts

4. How the website form works

The request form on this website does not store your information on our website. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in for you to review and send to us. We receive your information only if you choose to send it.

5. How we use your information and your consent

  • To respond to your enquiry and share suitable properties
  • To arrange site visits and share property documents
  • To prepare sale agreements, complete a purchase and support registration
  • To keep records required by law

We use your information only for these purposes, with your consent or where we need it to complete your purchase or meet the law. You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send you marketing without your consent.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to complete your purchase. This can include lawyers, surveyors, the Lands Commission and other authorities where the law requires it, and your appointed representative. We require the people we work with to keep your information confidential.

7. Information sent outside Ghana

WhatsApp, provided by Meta, may store our messages on servers outside Ghana, such as in the United States. If you live abroad, our messages and documents travel between Ghana and your country. When information is outside Ghana, it may be subject to the laws of that country.

8. Cookies and third parties

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not use analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address. WhatsApp handles messages under its own privacy policy.

9. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a purchase: up to 24 months
  • Sale agreements, payment records and related documents: at least 6 years, or longer where the law or a land matter requires it

After that, we delete or destroy it securely.

10. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Only people who need your information can see it, and we protect our phones and accounts with strong passwords and two-step verification. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, however.

11. If there is a data breach

If your personal data is accessed or obtained by someone who should not have it, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as soon as reasonably possible, as Ghana's law requires, and tell you what you can do to protect yourself.

12. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we hold personal data about you, see it, correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, subject to legal limits. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond within 21 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact Ghana's Data Protection Commission. If you live in Canada, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
