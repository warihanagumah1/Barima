About Barima Holdings

Everyday needs, handled by people you can trust.

Barima started with a simple idea: the things that matter most, the car you drive, the home you own and the food you share, deserve honest people behind them.

Today Barima Holdings serves families and businesses in Ghana, Canada and the United States through three companies. Each has its own team and expertise, and all of them share the Barima name and the standard that comes with it.

The Barima promise

What the name stands for

Honest by default

Clear costs on every vehicle, clean titles on every property, and no surprises after you have paid.

One person to call

A real contact from first call to finish, by phone, email or WhatsApp, in Ghana or in Canada.

At home on both sides

We know Ghana and we know Canada, so buying across the ocean feels as simple as buying next door.
