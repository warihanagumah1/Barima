FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Get in touch and we'll help.

Do you buy new and used vehicles?

Yes, both. Tell us the make, model, year and budget, and we'll find the best match through insurance auctions or the open market.

Are insurance auction vehicles damaged?

Some may have a history of damage or repairs. Before you commit, we share all the information available on the vehicle so you can decide with confidence.

How much is the duty in Ghana?

It depends on the vehicle's value, age and engine size, and it's set by the Ghana Revenue Authority, not by us. Ask us and we'll tell you what to expect before you commit.

How far ahead should I book a B-Drive ride?

As early as you can, especially for airport and railway station trips. For a same-day ride, call us on 416 605 6280.
