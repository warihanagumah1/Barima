FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Kindly call us and we'll talk it through.

What does "litigation-free" mean?

There is no court case, ownership dispute or competing claim on the land or property. Once you buy it, nobody else has a claim to it.

Do you sell land, houses and apartments?

Yes, all three. Tell us whether you want a plot, a house or an apartment, plus your area and budget, and we'll share what's available.

Can I check the documents before I pay?

Yes, and we encourage it. You or your lawyer can review them, and we recommend a search at the Lands Commission.

Which documents should I ask for?

Ask for the indenture and site plan, and the land title certificate where one exists. Then do a search at the Lands Commission to confirm who owns the land.

Can I visit the property first?

Of course. We arrange site visits, or someone you trust can visit on your behalf.

I live outside Ghana. Can I still buy?

Yes. Many Ghanaians abroad buy at home. Call or WhatsApp +233 54 858 0304 and we'll guide you through each step, including video tours of the property.
