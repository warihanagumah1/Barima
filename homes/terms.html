2. Our services

Barima Homes offers litigation-free land, houses and apartments for sale in Ghana. By litigation-free, we mean that, to our knowledge and after our own checks, there is no court case, ownership dispute or competing claim on the property at the time we offer it.

3. If a dispute arises after you buy

We will work with you to resolve it, and the remedy, such as a replacement plot or a refund, will be as set out in your sale agreement. Please read that part of your agreement carefully before you sign.

4. Property information

Descriptions, sizes, prices and availability of properties are given in good faith but may change, and they do not form a binding offer. A sale goes ahead only on the terms set out in a written agreement signed by both parties, as Ghana's Land Act, 2020 (Act 1036) requires.

5. Your own checks

We share the property documents before payment, and we encourage every buyer to check them independently. We strongly recommend that you, or your lawyer, conduct a search at the Lands Commission and visit the site before you pay. Your decision to buy is your own.

6. Buyers who are not Ghanaian citizens

Under Ghana's Constitution and the Land Act, 2020, a person who is not a Ghanaian citizen cannot own land outright (freehold). Non-citizens can hold land on a lease of up to 50 years, which may be renewed. If you are not a Ghanaian citizen, including if you hold only Canadian or another foreign citizenship, your sale agreement will grant a lease on these terms. Ghanaians with dual citizenship keep their rights as Ghanaian citizens. Please tell us your citizenship before you commit, and take legal advice if you are unsure.

Make payments only through the methods and accounts named in your written agreement. We will never change our payment details by WhatsApp or phone alone. If anyone asks you to pay a different account, call us on 054 858 0304 before paying.
